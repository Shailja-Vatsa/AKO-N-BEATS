# AKO-N-BEATS

A lightweight command-line music player designed to organize and play local audio tracks directly from your terminal.

---

## Features

- **Lightweight & Fast:** Minimal resource overhead with instant playback.
- **Terminal-Based UI:** Navigate and manage music without leaving your terminal.
- **Playlist & Queue Management:** Easily queue tracks, loop, and shuffle playlists.
- **Broad Format Support:** Plays common audio formats (MP3, WAV, FLAC, etc.).

---
